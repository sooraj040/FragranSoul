Background music
================

Put one music file here named "background.mp3" and the store plays it softly
behind every page, starting 26 seconds in (change BACKGROUND_MUSIC_START in
fragransoul/settings.py to start somewhere else). When the track ends it
begins again from that starting point.

Only use music you have the right to play on a public website: a track you
made, one you bought a licence for, or royalty-free music. A song from a
streaming service or YouTube is not licensed for this.

With no file here, the store plays a gentle built-in tone instead.
